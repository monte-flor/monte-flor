The Story

From Dutch to Galician

After twenty years growing heritage flowers in the Netherlands, I fell in love with the microclimates, granitic soils, and wild morning mists of Galicia's Ribeira Sacra.

Here on terraced slopes carved by monks centuries ago, we grow specialty cut flowers without synthetic fertilizers, heated glasshouses, or chemical sprays — working strictly with rain, compost, and the rhythm of Atlantic seasons.

 Volunteering

Come Help

We welcome budding growers, florists, and nature lovers to live and work alongside Annemiek on the farm. Learn low-till cut flower techniques in exchange for lodging and fresh farm meals.

compost

1. Soil & Composting

Deep-dive into microbial soil health, hot composting with local oak bark, and building organic raised beds without turning subsoil.

potted_plant

2. Seed Propagation

Soil blocking, cold stratification, winter sowing, and microclimate care for finicky specialty blooms and perennial seed saving.

content_cut

3. Harvesting Technique

Dawn harvests at the optimal stage of opening to guarantee exceptional vase life, straight stems, and continual plant regeneration.

science

4. Natural Conditioning

Post-harvest hydrology, resting cut stems in spring water, and chemical-free storage techniques that preserve color and fragrance.
